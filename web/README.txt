LearnCpp

Windows x64: double-click LearnCpp.exe.
The app opens http://127.0.0.1:8768/ in your default browser.
The native C++ backend is already compiled and needs no additional runtime installation.
It exits a few seconds after the last LearnCpp tab closes. Refreshing and switching lessons keep it running.

Progress and highlights are saved automatically to data/state.json.
data/state.json.bak is the previous saved version.
Wait until the page says Saved, close LearnCpp, then copy the entire folder to another Windows device.
Double-click LearnCpp.exe on that device to continue reading.
Opening index.html directly allows reading but cannot save or restore folder data.

Records from the older browser-storage edition are not imported automatically.
